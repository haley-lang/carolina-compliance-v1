## "Worth a quick look" — Billy/RTR batch

Before this session's fixes, **51 of 56** Billy/RTR records triggered "Worth a quick look."

Two causes were corrected:

1. **`possible_duplicate` over-flagging** — old logic flagged every record sharing the same
   normalized Named Insured (Counter-based). New logic requires an identical
   `(normalized_policy_number, effective_date)` pair in addition to matching NI.
   Result: 37 → 4 records flagged (2 confirmed real pairs verified against live Airtable).

2. **`changed_vs_prior` driving the count** — this flag was included in `needs_look`
   computation. Demoted to informational display only (italic, no pill).
   Result: the flag still appears in the queue row for Haley's awareness, but no longer
   counts toward "Worth a quick look."

**Row-count note (58 vs 54):** The RTR batch filter matches on
`Source Filename` containing `"rtr"` (case-insensitive). Four filenames beginning
`N9WC394833-ACORDAPP25-I` and `coi.json` are from a multi-page upload whose pages
were assigned those names rather than the `RTR_` prefix. Those 4 rows appear under
"Everything else" not "Billy / RTR," reducing the RTR count to 54 from a raw 58.
